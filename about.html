About FakeNews AI

FakeNews AI is an AI-powered web application designed to help users verify the authenticity of news articles and headlines. The system analyzes the submitted content and predicts whether it is likely to be Real or Fake, along with a confidence score and explanation.

AI Analysis

Uses machine learning and natural language processing techniques to analyze news content.

Reliable Detection

Detects misleading information using trusted AI models and confidence scoring.

Analytics

Provides insights, prediction history, and detection statistics.

Privacy First

User-submitted news is processed securely to protect privacy and maintain confidentiality.

Our Mission

To combat misinformation by providing users with a simple, fast, and intelligent platform for verifying the credibility of online news and helping promote trustworthy information.
